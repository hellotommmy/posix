\section{The one inequality we must prove}
The D law, the static cubic front, and the gate wrapper are all green. The \S1 gate reduces to
the single numeric inequality
\[
\boxed{\ \rss{\sd\;p\;k}\ \le\ \ctxb(\dctx\;p)\;k\ }\qquad\text{(TRUE: $0$ violations over $>10^5$ depth-$\ge5$ cases).}
\]
Everything below is about \emph{proving} this true inequality. Four/eight routes failed; this
note shows, for each, the exact left/right sides and their values on the counterexamples.

\section{The objects (concrete)}
\begin{description}[leftmargin=1.2em,itemsep=2pt]
\item[$\Strong$ (\texttt{rsimpStrong\_raw}).] The strong normaliser. Crucially it \emph{collapses}
  $a^{*}\seq a^{*}\to a^{*}$ (a guarded rule, only when the two starred bodies are syntactically equal).
\item[$\rfour$ (\texttt{rsimp4\_SEQ\_atom}).] The \emph{weak} sequence plug: $h\rfour k$ appends $k$
  after head $h$ (reassociating), but \textbf{never} collapses $a^{*}\seq a^{*}$. (\texttt{rsimp7} is
  the same but \emph{does} collapse, at the top head only.)
\item[$\dctx\;p$.] A \emph{list} of slots $(\textit{head}_i,\textit{cost}_i)$, one per ``drain weight''
  unit of $p$ (one per character, one per star), built structurally.
  $\textit{slot\_cost}(p,k,i)=\textit{cost}_i+(1+\rs{k})$ and $\ctxb(\dctx p)\,k=\sum_i \textit{slot\_cost}(p,k,i)$.
\item[$\textit{weak\_slot\_rows}(p,k,i) = \mathrm{rows}(\,\textit{head}_i\rfour k\,)\setminus\mathrm{rows}(k)$.]
  The \emph{uncollapsed} opening of slot $i$. $\wcd\;p\;k=\bigcup_i \textit{weak\_slot\_rows}(p,k,i)$.
  Green fact: $\rss{\wcd\;p\;k}\le \ctxb(\dctx p)\,k$.
\item[$\sd\;p\;k = \SOL(\textit{nseq}\;p\;k)\setminus\SOL(\Strong k)$.] The \textbf{actual} object:
  the opened rows after the \emph{strong}, $\Strong$-normalised step. \emph{These rows are collapsed.}
\end{description}
\noindent\textbf{The whole tension in one line:} the weak rows use $\rfour$ (uncollapsed), the strong
rows use $\Strong$ (collapsed). So a strong row and ``its'' weak row are often \emph{different rows} of
\emph{different sizes}, even though the budget covers them.

\section{Three counterexamples, fully computed}
All three use $k=a^{*}$, and all are in-regime ($\Strong p=p,\ \Strong k=k$, normal form). For each
$p$ the slot ledger $\dctx p$ and $\ctxb$ are shown, then $\sd$ and $\wcd$.

\subsection*{CE-A: $p=b\seq a^{*}$ --- the collapse, in isolation}
\[
\sd(b\seq a^{*},\,a^{*}) = \{\,\underbrace{b\seq a^{*}}_{\rs{}=4}\,\},\qquad \rss{\sd}=4.
\]
Slots of $\dctx(b\seq a^{*})$ (head, cost, slot\_cost, weak row):
\[
\begin{array}{llll}
\text{slot}_0: & b\seq a^{*}\ (4) & \textit{slot\_cost}=7 & \mathrm{weak}=b\seq(a^{*}\seq a^{*})\ \ \rs{}=7\\
\text{slot}_1: & a^{*}\ (2) & \textit{slot\_cost}=5 & \mathrm{weak}=a^{*}\seq a^{*}\ \ \rs{}=5\\
\text{slot}_2: & a\seq a^{*}\ (4) & \textit{slot\_cost}=7 & \mathrm{weak}=a\seq(a^{*}\seq a^{*})\ \ \rs{}=7
\end{array}
\]
$\ctxb=7+5+7=19$, and $\wcd=\{b\seq(a^{*}\seq a^{*}),\ a^{*}\seq a^{*},\ a\seq(a^{*}\seq a^{*})\}$, $\rss{\wcd}=19$.
\textbf{Key:} the one strong row $b\seq a^{*}$ is the \emph{$\Strong$-collapse} of the slot$_0$ weak row:
\[
\Strong\bigl(b\seq(a^{*}\seq a^{*})\bigr)=b\seq a^{*},\qquad \rs{b\seq a^{*}}=4\ \le\ 7=\rs{b\seq(a^{*}\seq a^{*})}.
\]
So $b\seq a^{*}\notin\wcd$ (set-containment into the weak cover \emph{fails}), but it is the collapse of a
weak row, and collapse \emph{shrinks}. Bound holds: $4\le19$.

\subsection*{CE-B: $p=b\seq a^{*}\alt\one$ --- the RALTS wrap \emph{retains} the uncollapsed row}
Same slots/$\ctxb=19$ as CE-A (the $\one$ adds no slot). But now
\[
\sd(b\seq a^{*}\alt\one,\,a^{*}) = \{\,\underbrace{b\seq(a^{*}\seq a^{*})}_{\rs{}=7}\,\},\qquad \rss{\sd}=7 .
\]
\textbf{Same head $b\seq a^{*}$, opposite outcome:} wrapped under $\alt$, the opener exposes the
\emph{uncollapsed} $b\seq(a^{*}\seq a^{*})$ and the strong step does \emph{not} collapse it back.
And $\Strong$ runs the \emph{wrong way} for any witness:
\[
\Strong\bigl(b\seq(a^{*}\seq a^{*})\bigr)=b\seq a^{*}\ \neq\ b\seq(a^{*}\seq a^{*}).
\]
So \emph{no} weak row $y$ has $\Strong(y)=$ the strong row (every $\Strong$-image is the \emph{shorter}
$b\seq a^{*}$). The strong row here \emph{is} the slot$_0$ weak row itself ($x=y$). Bound holds: $7\le19$.

\subsection*{CE-Hall: $p=a\alt(b^{*}\seq a^{*})$ --- both versions at once}
\[
\sd(a\alt b^{*}\seq a^{*},\,a^{*})=\{\,\underbrace{a\seq a^{*}}_{4},\ \underbrace{b^{*}\seq a^{*}}_{5},\ \underbrace{b^{*}\seq(a^{*}\seq a^{*})}_{8}\,\},\quad \rss{\sd}=17,\quad \ctxb=34 .
\]
The slot for head $b^{*}\seq a^{*}$ is slot$_1$ (cost $5$, slot\_cost $8$, weak row $b^{*}\seq(a^{*}\seq a^{*})$, size $8$). Now:
\[
b^{*}\seq(a^{*}\seq a^{*})\ \text{is the slot}_1\ \text{weak row }(x{=}y),\qquad
\Strong\bigl(b^{*}\seq(a^{*}\seq a^{*})\bigr)=b^{*}\seq a^{*}.
\]
So \emph{both} strong rows $b^{*}\seq a^{*}$ (collapsed, $5$) and $b^{*}\seq(a^{*}\seq a^{*})$ (uncollapsed, $8$)
have their \emph{only} origin at slot$_1$, whose single budget $8$ cannot pay both ($5+8=13>8$). Bound still
holds ($17\le34$): the \emph{total} budget covers them because other slots are underused.

\section{Each failed route: left side, right side, value on the CE}
\begin{description}[leftmargin=0pt,itemsep=5pt]

\item[\textbf{R1. Per-child set-containment} (\texttt{master\_cover}, the original C-DRAIN).]
Tried: $\ \sd(\textstyle\sum_q q)\,k\ \subseteq\ \bigcup_q \sd\;q\;k\ $ (parent rows are union of child rows).
\emph{Intuition:} a $\sum$ should just be the union of its parts.
\emph{Killed by CE-B:} $\ \mathrm{LHS}=\{b\seq(a^{*}\seq a^{*})\}\ $ (parent, uncollapsed), but the child
$b\seq a^{*}$ alone gives $\sd(b\seq a^{*},a^{*})=\{b\seq a^{*}\}$ (CE-A, collapsed) and $\one$ gives $\{\}$, so
$\ \mathrm{RHS}=\{b\seq a^{*}\}$. The parent's uncollapsed row $b\seq(a^{*}\seq a^{*})\notin\{b\seq a^{*}\}$. The
$\alt$-wrap blocks the collapse the child performs. Fails.

\item[\textbf{R2. Total-size domination.}]
Tried: $\ \rss{\sd\;p\;k}\ \le\ \rss{\wcd\;p\;k}\ $ (then the green $\rss{\wcd}\le\ctxb$ finishes).
\emph{Intuition:} strong rows are ``smaller'' than the weak cover, so their total is too.
\emph{False:} $\Strong$ can \emph{grow} a row's opened ledger, so the strong total can exceed the weak total.
Machine CE: $p=(\,\one\alt b\seq(a\alt\one)\alt(\one\alt a)\seq a^{*}\,)\seq a^{*},\ k=c$:
$\ \rss{\sd}=36\ >\ 29=\rss{\wcd}\ $ (while $\ctxb=51$ still covers $36$). So the weak \emph{set} does not dominate;
only the \emph{ledger} over-covers.

\item[\textbf{R3. Injective slot-origin via \texttt{collapses\_to}} (verdict6).]
Tried: an injection $x\mapsto(\text{slot } i_x,\ \text{witness } y_x)$ with $\ \Strong(y_x)=\{x\}\ $ and $\rs{x}\le\rs{y_x}$.
\emph{Intuition:} each strong row is the collapse of a paid weak slot row.
\emph{Killed by CE-B:} the strong row $x=b\seq(a^{*}\seq a^{*})$ is \emph{uncollapsed}; no weak $y$ has
$\Strong(y)=x$ ($\Strong$ only \emph{produces} the shorter $b\seq a^{*}$). The relation points the wrong way:
$x$ is the long predecessor, not the collapse. ($97/40849$ failures.)

\item[\textbf{R4. Two-route injective charge} (secretary salvage).]
Tried: same injection but $\ x=y_x\ \vee\ x\in\mathrm{rows}(\Strong y_x)\ $ (allow ``$x$ \emph{is} the weak row'').
\emph{Intuition:} cover both the uncollapsed ($x{=}y$) and collapsed ($x=\Strong y$) cases.
\emph{Killed by CE-Hall:} $b^{*}\seq a^{*}$ (via $x\in\Strong y$) and $b^{*}\seq(a^{*}\seq a^{*})$ (via $x{=}y$) are
\emph{both} edged only to slot$_1$. Two rows, one slot $\Rightarrow$ no injection (a Hall collision), even though
slot$_1$'s cost would suffice for either alone. ($24/52851$; cuts R3 by $4\times$ but cannot reach $0$.)

\item[\textbf{R5. Pure numeric induction, RALTS step.}]
Tried: $\ \rss{\sd(\sum_q q)\,k}\ \le\ \sum_q \rss{\sd\;q\;k}\ $ (deduped-union size bounded by the sum of children).
\emph{Killed by CE-B:} $\ \mathrm{LHS}=\rss{\{b\seq(a^{*}\seq a^{*})\}}=7\ $, $\ \mathrm{RHS}=\rss{\{b\seq a^{*}\}}+0=4\ $;
$7>4$. The parent's single uncollapsed row is \emph{bigger} than the whole child sum. (RALTS has zero
constructor slack, so there is no room for the extra.)

\item[\textbf{R6. Any slot-derived carrier (the impossibility square).}]
For \emph{any} carrier $W'$ built from the slot rows: plain weak $W'=\wcd$ has $\rss{\sd}>\rss{W'}$ sometimes (R2);
$\Strong$-applied carriers shrink \emph{below} $\sd$ (bridge fails worse); $\wcd\cup\sd$ pushes the \emph{ceiling}
above $\ctxb$ (CE-A-like: $23>19$). No carrier sits between $\rss{\sd}$ and $\ctxb$. The whole
carrier/membership class is dead.
\end{description}

\section{The one obstruction, stated once}
$\sd\;p\;k$ is a \emph{heterogeneous} set: a $\sum$/star wrap can make it \emph{retain the uncollapsed long
row} $b\seq(a^{*}\seq a^{*})$ (CE-B), while a bare sequence makes it \emph{keep the collapsed short row}
$b\seq a^{*}$ (CE-A), and a mix keeps \emph{both} (CE-Hall). The weak cover only ever holds the uncollapsed
forms. Therefore:
\begin{itemize}[itemsep=1pt]
\item set-containment fails (collapsed strong rows are not weak members);
\item $\Strong$-witnessing fails (uncollapsed strong rows are not $\Strong$-images);
\item any row$\to$slot \emph{injection} fails (collapsed + uncollapsed siblings collide on one slot);
\item yet the \emph{total} ledger $\ctxb$ always covers $\rss{\sd}$ (other slots are underused).
\end{itemize}
\textbf{Consequence (the search-narrowing result):} the proof cannot be a per-row or per-slot
\emph{set/membership} statement. It must be a \emph{total-budget} argument that does not match rows to slots.

\section{Where verdict7 points}
verdict7's ``budgeted collapse trace'' is exactly this: charge at the level of \emph{syntax-node
occurrences} $\to$ \emph{cost tokens} (atoms of $\ctxb$), not rows $\to$ slots. On CE-Hall the short row
$b^{*}\seq a^{*}$ ($5$ nodes) and the long row $b^{*}\seq(a^{*}\seq a^{*})$ ($8$ nodes) consume
\emph{disjoint} cost atoms (drawn from slot$_1$ \emph{and} the source-star/collapse slot) instead of both
demanding the one indivisible slot$_1$ --- so the Hall collision dissolves. The open question is whether the
node$\to$atom injection is constructible at RALTS; that is what to validate before executing.
