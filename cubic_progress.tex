\begin{abstract}
This document is an incrementally updated ledger of the campaign to prove a
cubic size bound for the Antimirov-factored strong-simplification route of
the POSIX lexer formalization.  Every statement labelled \emph{checked} names
an Isabelle lemma that builds green in the \code{Posix} session; every
counterexample labelled \emph{checked} is likewise an Isabelle fact.
Mirror-validated statements (random testing against a clause-verified Python
model) are clearly marked as such and are \emph{not} proofs.  The document is
recompiled after each increment of progress.
\end{abstract}

\section{The target}

For the erased regular-expression datatype \code{rrexp} (constructors
\code{RZERO}, \code{RONE}, \code{RCHAR}, \code{RSEQ}, \code{RALTS},
\code{RSTAR}, \code{RNTIMES}, plus backreference constructors excluded from
the fragment), the live target is the \emph{set-ledger cubic gate}
(\code{MAINLINE.md}):
\[
\rsizeset\bigl(\code{row\_dlformss}\,(\code{rpder\_strong\_rows\_raw}\;c\;(\code{afactored1}\;r\;s))\bigr)
\;\le\; 2\,(\rsize r + 3)^3 .
\]
In words: take the rows produced by one strong derivative step from the
factored front of $r$ after input $s$, open every row into its linear forms,
deduplicate \emph{across the whole union}, and sum the sizes of the distinct
opened rows; this total must be cubic in the size of the original regex.
POSIX value preservation is handled by the separately checked
deferred-memo reconstruction theorems and is not re-proved here.

\section{Checked refutation: the duplicated list account is exponential}

The first reduction attempt measured the \emph{duplicated list} cost
$\code{afactored1\_strong\_dlform\_list\_cost}$ (sum of opened-list sizes,
duplicates counted).  This is provably hopeless.

\begin{theorem}[checked: \code{afactored1\_strong\_dlform\_list\_cost\_cubic\_false}]
There is a family $r_j$ with $\rsize r_j = \Theta(j)$ whose one-step
duplicated list cost grows as $2^j$; in particular the cost is not bounded by
$2(\rsize r+3)^3$, nor by any polynomial.
\end{theorem}

The witness is the \emph{RONE-pair tower}: with
$m' = \code{RSEQ}\,(\code{RALTS}[\code{RONE},a])\,(\code{RALTS}[\code{RONE},b])$
and $x_j = \code{RSEQ}\,(\code{RALTS}[\code{RONE},m'])\,x_{j-1}$, every
nesting level doubles the number of opened rows because the two
\code{RONE}-paths of $m'$ both reopen the tail; the tower is a literal
fixpoint of the strong simplifier, so simplification does not help.  All
fully collapsing \code{RONE}-paths produce \emph{the same} row, which is why
the deduplicated (set-level) account below survives.

\section{Checked set-level theory}

\begin{theorem}[checked: \code{card\_row\_dlforms\_le\_rsize}]
For every row $q$: $\,|\code{row\_dlforms}\,q| \le \rsize q$.  (Distinct
opened rows of a single row are at most linear, unconditionally.)
\end{theorem}

The proof is a set-difference triangle along the reassociation chain; the
core checked inequality is
\code{card\_row\_dlforms\_rsimp4\_diff\_le}:
$|\code{row\_dlforms}(\sigma_4(p,k)) \setminus \code{row\_dlforms}\,k| \le \rsize p$,
where $\sigma_4$ is the sequence-atom smart constructor.

\begin{theorem}[checked: \code{rsize\_set\_row\_dlforms\_le\_rsize\_sq}]
$\rsizeset(\code{row\_dlforms}\,q) \le (\rsize q)^2$ for every row $q$.
\end{theorem}

\begin{theorem}[checked: \code{card\_row\_dlformss\_le\_rsizes} and
\code{card\_row\_dlformss\_rpder\_strong\_rows\_raw\_le\_generated}]
The deduplicated union over a row list has at most $\rsizes(\textit{rows})$
members; instantiated to the actual one-step output, the union card is at
most the generated total size (one degree).
\end{theorem}

\section{Checked gate reduction: from three summands to two numbers}

The supervisor's pair-budget gate splits the union ledger into a non-sequence
part, a front-times-tail-weights part, and an active-suffix pair-budget part.
Both abstract premises of the gate were discharged
(\code{actual\_union\_seq\_head\_size\_linear} gives head bound
$H = \mathrm{Suc}(2\,\rsize r)$ on the legacy fragment;
\code{rseq\_tails\_row\_dlformss\_rpder\_strong\_weight\_le\_generated} gives
the key weight $M$), yielding the instantiated gate
\code{actual\_union\_pair\_budget\_gate\_instance}.  Then:

\begin{theorem}[checked: \code{pair\_budget\_afactored1\_strong\_dlform\_universe\_zero}]
The active-suffix pair budget of the dlform universe is identically zero:
opened rows always have non-alternation heads
(\code{row\_dlforms\_seq\_member\_head\_nonalt}), so no member of the
universe has the keyed shape $\code{RSEQ}\,(\code{RALTS}\,\textit{rows})\,k$.
The third gate summand vanishes
(\code{actual\_union\_gate\_two\_summands}).
\end{theorem}

\begin{theorem}[checked: exact decomposition]
With \code{rsize\_set\_split\_rnonseq\_rseq},
\code{rsize\_set\_rseq\_members\_le\_heads\_tails} and
\code{sum\_rseq\_member\_tails\_regroup}, the whole gate rests on exactly two
numbers for the actual opened union $U$:
\[
\rsizeset U \;\le\; \underbrace{\text{nonseq part}}_{\text{cubic, checked}}
\;+\; |{\code{rseq\_members}\,U}| \cdot \mathrm{Suc}\,H
\;+\; \sum_{t} |\code{rseq\_tail\_rows}\,U\,t| \cdot \rsize t .
\]
The non-sequence part is cubic by
\code{rsize\_set\_rnonseq\_members\_\dots\_cubic} via the front-terms carrier.
\end{theorem}

\section{Checked static universe results}

\begin{theorem}[checked: \code{rsizes\_afactored1\_le\_rsize\_set\_apder\_rows}]
For \emph{every} input $s$:
$\rsizes(\code{afactored1}\,r\,s) \le \rsizeset(\code{apder\_rows}\,r)$.
The multi-step front total is bounded by a static, input-independent
quantity (rows are distinct and live in the static row universe).
\end{theorem}

\begin{theorem}[checked: \code{apder\_rows\_member\_size\_quadratic}]
On the \code{apder\_nf} fragment every member of the static row universe has
size at most $\mathrm{Suc}((\rsize r + 2)^2)$.  Hence every dynamic front row
is at most quadratic --- the row-size half of the front-quadratic conjecture.
\end{theorem}

\begin{counterexample}[mirror-verified, tight]
The static ledger $\rsizeset(\code{apder\_rows}\,r)$ is \emph{cubic-tight}:
on the star-seq towers $x_{i+1} = (x_i\, b_i)^{*}$ the ratio
$\rsizeset/(\rsize)^3$ converges to $\approx 0.040$.  The checked cubic bound
\code{rsize\_set\_apder\_rows\_expanded\_cubic\_size\_bound} cannot be
improved to quadratic.  Moreover the \emph{reachable} row universe equals the
static one on this family, so no set-shaped (history-independent) bound can
beat cubic; the missing degree is irreducibly about rows \emph{coexisting} in
one front (liveness).
\end{counterexample}

\section{The row-count law (the ``D law'')}

The remaining static improvement is row-\emph{count} linearity, classical
Antimirov shape.  Write $\acc r k$ for
\code{apder\_term\_frontier\_acc}, $\F k$ for \code{rfrontier}\,$k$.

\begin{definition}[checked: \code{apder\_zw2}]
$\zw_2(\code{RCHAR}) = 1$; sums over \code{RALTS}/\code{RSEQ};
$\zw_2(\code{RSTAR}\,x) = 1 + \zw_2(x)$;
$\zw_2(\code{RNTIMES}\,r\,n) = n\,(1+\zw_2 r)$; $0$ otherwise.
Checked: $\zw_2 \le \rsize$ on the \code{rntimes\_free} fragment
(\code{apder\_zw2\_rntimes\_free\_le\_rsize}).
\end{definition}

\begin{conjecture}[the D law, \code{rntimes\_free} form; mirror: $>295{,}000$ deep samples, zero violations]
\label{conj:dlaw}
On the legacy, \code{apder\_nf}, \code{rntimes\_free} fragment:
\[
|\acc r k \setminus \F k| \;\le\; \zw_2\, r .
\]
\end{conjecture}

\subsection{Checked falsifications along the way}

\begin{counterexample}[checked and mirror; weight too small]
The plain letter-count (\code{apder\_awidth}) version is false:
zero-width stars produce frontier rows without letters.
The first corrected weight (\code{apder\_zwidth}, star $=\max 1$) is
\emph{also} false at depth:
$r = b\,(\code{ALTS}[(a^{*})^{*}, (b^{*})^{*}, b])$, $k=\code{RONE}$ gives
$5 > 4$ --- each nested zero-consuming star layer needs its own slot.
\end{counterexample}

\begin{counterexample}[checked: \code{apder\_zw2\_D\_law\_rntimes\_zero\_alt\_false}]
The unrestricted $\zw_2$ law is false with zero counted repetitions:
$r = c\,(\code{ALTS}[\code{RNTIMES}\,a\,0, \code{RNTIMES}\,b\,0])$ has
two imported frontier atoms but $\zw_2 r = 1$.  Hence the
\code{rntimes\_free} restriction in Conjecture~\ref{conj:dlaw}.
\end{counterexample}

Nine inductive strengthenings (subset/intersect discounts, membership
companions, potential and reserve forms, an undeduplicated list-length form,
and the first joint invariant $J^*$) were falsified by explicit
counterexamples; the full list with witnesses is maintained in
\code{MATHPROBLEM\_ROWCOUNT.md}.  A hard methodological lesson is recorded
there: two false statements each passed $\ge 95{,}000$ \emph{shallow} random
samples; all sampling now requires depth $\ge 5$ plus directed
nested-star/zero-width families.

\subsection{Constructor bridges (checked, supervisor)}

The current attack decomposes the D law by head constructor.  Checked
bridges so far include the \code{RSEQ} frontier singleton shifts, the
$\sigma_4$ non-\code{RONE} preservation and frontier-shape lemmas, the
normal-nonalt frontier/carry-bucket shifts, the right-nonalt SEQ
D bridge, and its \code{RCHAR}-left specialization
(\code{card\_apder\_term\_frontier\_acc\_RSEQ\_RCHAR\_diff\_le\_if\_right\_nonalt\_diff}):
an ordinary D bound for an \code{apder\_nf} non-alternation right child
yields the exact parent SEQ bound when the left child is a character.

\subsection{Payoff when landed}

$|\code{apder\_rows}\,r| \le \zw_2 r + 2$, hence with the checked quadratic
member bound: $\rsizes(\code{afactored1}\,r\,s) \le (\zw_2 r + 2) \cdot
\mathrm{Suc}((\rsize r+2)^2)$ --- a \emph{cubic static front bound by
assembly} on the fragment, replacing the tight-ledger detour, and the
row-count half of the dynamic front-quadratic conjecture.

\section{The liveness frontier}

All single-step syntactic quantities are now known cubic-tight
(static ledger, per-member size, per-row norm ledgers --- each with explicit
witness families).  The remaining degree for the full gate is the
\emph{liveness slice}: simultaneously live rows are a quadratic slice of the
cubic static universe.  Mirror-validated with zero violations across all
families is the cap-law invariant
\[
\rsizes(\code{afactored\_step}\,c\;\textit{rows}) \;\le\;
\max\bigl(\rsizes\,\textit{rows},\; C\,(\rsize r)^2\bigr),
\]
factored into three single-step obligations (jump bound, monotone
self-preservation, jump saturation) --- the first formulation in which every
obligation quantifies over one front and one step.  Formalization has not
started.
