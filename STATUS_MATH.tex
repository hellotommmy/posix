{\large\textbf{Where the cubic-bound proof is right now}}\hfill\textsf{\small 2026-06-14 11:20}

\medskip
Working fragment: classical regexes, in normal form, no bounded repetition
$r^{\{n\}}$ (the ``clean'' fragment). Everything below is machine-checked in
Isabelle with no gaps unless marked $[\rightarrow]$ or $[\circ]$.

\bigskip
\textbf{The goal} (one inequality). Run the lexer's normalized derivative
automaton from $r$ over input $s$, do \emph{one} strongly-simplified step on the
next letter $c$, open every resulting row into linear forms and deduplicate,
and sum their sizes. That total is cubic in $\rs{r}$:
\[
\boxed{\;\norm{\;\Open\bigl(\Strong_c(\Front(r,s))\bigr)\;}\;\le\;2\,(\rs{r}+3)^3\;}
\]
uniformly in $s$ (its length does not appear on the right).

\bigskip
\textbf{Notation.} $\rs{r}$ = size of $r$;\;
$\acc(r,k)$ = rows the derivative of $r$ leaves in front of continuation $k$;\;
$\front k$ = one-step frontier of $k$;\;
$\seqn$ = sequence normalizer;\;
$\zw(r)$ = weight (letters, $+1$ per star) — always $\zw(r)\le\rs{r}$;\;
$\Rows(r)$ = all rows reachable from $r$ (static);\;
$\Front(r,s)$ = the normalized front after reading $s$;\;
$\Strong_c$ = one strong simplify+prune step;\;
$\Open$ = open rows to linear forms and dedup.

\bigskip
\hrule
\medskip
\textbf{The chain (top to bottom). Read it as: each line is justified by the one below it.}

\medskip
\begin{enumerate}[leftmargin=2.2em,itemsep=6pt]

\item[\done] \textbf{Goal, on the clean fragment.}
$\norm{\Open(\Strong_c(\Front(r,s)))}$ splits into a non-sequence part
(already cubic) plus a sequence part bounded by the front total $\norm{\Front(r,s)}$.
\lem{actual\_union\_gate\_two\_summands; rsize\_set\_rnonseq\_members\_\dots\_cubic}

\item[\done] \textbf{Static front is cubic, for every input.}
\[
\norm{\Front(r,s)}\;\le\;(\rs{r}+3)^3\qquad\text{for all }s.
\]
\emph{In words:} however long the input, the normalized front never exceeds a
fixed cubic in the original size.
\lem{rsizes\_afactored1\_clean\_static\_cubic}

\item[\done] \textbf{It rests on two facts about the static row universe:}
\[
\#\,\Rows(r)\;\le\;\rs{r}+2
\qquad\text{and}\qquad
\text{every row in }\Rows(r)\text{ has size}\;\le\;(\rs{r}+2)^2 .
\]
\emph{In words:} few rows (linearly many), each small (quadratic). Linear
$\times$ quadratic $=$ cubic.
\lem{card\_apder\_rows\_clean\_le\_rsize\_plus\_2;\ apder\_rows\_member\_size\_quadratic}

\item[\done] \textbf{The row count is linear because of the D law} (the hard part,
cracked via the GPT-Pro ``telescoping'' invariant):
\[
\#\bigl(\acc(r,k)\setminus\front k\bigr)\;\le\;\zw(r)
\qquad\text{(clean }r,k\text{).}
\]
\emph{In words:} one derivative step adds at most $\zw(r)\ (\le\rs{r})$ genuinely
new rows. Proved by a single simultaneous induction on the stronger boundary
invariant $\#((\front(\seqn r k)\cup\acc(r,k))\setminus\front k)\le\zw(r)$ plus a
strict-credit lemma.
\lem{D\_law\_clean\ (from\ apder\_T\_bound/apder\_S\_bound\ via\ T\_and\_S)}

\item[\done] \textbf{The D law applies to the \emph{actual} rows.}
The rows the gate feeds are in the clean domain.
\lem{apder\_clean\_apder\_rows;\ apder\_clean\_afactored1}

\item[\now] \textbf{The one remaining step: glue the cubic front into the gate.}
Push $\norm{\Front(r,s)}\le(\rs{r}+3)^3$ through the single strong step, the
opening, and the dedup
\[
\Front(r,s)\ \xrightarrow{\ \Strong_c\ }\ \cdot\ \xrightarrow{\ \Open\ }\ \cdot
\ \xrightarrow{\ \norm{\,\cdot\,}\ }\ \le\ 2(\rs{r}+3)^3,
\]
and discharge the ``clean'' hypothesis for the actual root.
\textbf{Design point, status 2026-06-14 (post-refutation).} The cubic front does not
reach the gate by naive assembly (the per-row square-sum is quintic; the non-dedup
list is exponential --- RONE-pair tower). The viable carrier is the \textbf{drain
carrier over the current NORMALIZED rows} ($\mathrm{strong\_opened\_live}$): its
$\S5$ \emph{arithmetic} is DONE/green ($\mathrm{drain\_pot\_le\_cubic\_core}$), and
the $\S4$ per-child SET-containment is PROVEN for SEQ/CHAR. \emph{But the per-child
telescoping inclusion is now machine/sample-checked FALSE for RALTS and RSTAR}, with
the mechanism secretary-verified against the real Isabelle definitions: a guarded
rule $\mathrm{rsimp7\_SEQ\_atom}(a^{*},a^{*})=a^{*}$ collapses $a^{*}\!\cdot\!a^{*}\!\to\!a^{*}$
in a child while $\mathrm{rsimp4\_SEQ\_atom}$ keeps it, so the parent opened row holds
a bigger form no child drain contains (CE\,C-DRAIN-1 $r=(b\,a^{*}{+}1)$, $k=a^{*}$;
CE\,C-DRAIN-2 $r=((1{+}a)c)^{*}$, $k=1$). Adding the full parent opened row as a
boundary makes the inclusion HOLD but cannot be PAID: $\mathrm{RALTS}$ has zero
constructor slack ($\open\_pot$ and $\zw$ of $\mathrm{RALTS}$ are exactly $\Sigma$
over children). The master bound itself is still believed TRUE ($3{\times}10^{5}$
samples). \emph{Open design question} (GPT Pro pass, in preparation): a
\textbf{non-telescoping ALTS/STAR account} that bounds the parent drain directly
within budget, reusing the proven SEQ/CHAR containment and the green $\S5$
arithmetic. See \texttt{GATE\_BRIDGE\_GAP.md} UPDATE~3. Once it lands, the boxed
goal closes (clean fragment).

\item[\todo] \textbf{After that:} lift the result from the clean fragment to
\emph{all} regexes including bounded repetition $r^{\{n\}}$. This needs a
repetition-aware weight (the plain $\zw$ fails when a $\{0\}$ block carries an
empty-but-present frontier). Optional; may not be required for the thesis target.

\end{enumerate}

\medskip
\textbf{The symbols, defined} (so the inequalities above are self-contained).
Throughout, $r,k$ are regexes, $\Sigma rs$ a list-alternation, $c$ a letter.

\begin{itemize}[leftmargin=1.4em,itemsep=4pt]

\item \textbf{Size} $\rs{r}$:\;\,
$\rs{0}=\rs{1}=\rs{c}=1$,\;
$\rs{r_1 r_2}=1+\rs{r_1}+\rs{r_2}$,\;
$\rs{\Sigma rs}=1+\textstyle\sum_i\rs{r_i}$,\;
$\rs{r^*}=1+\rs{r}$,\;
$\rs{r^{\{n\}}}=1+\rs{r}+n$.

\item \textbf{Frontier} $\front k$ --- the rows of $k$ after splitting its top
alternation:\;
$\front 0=\emptyset$,\;\,
$\front(\Sigma rs)=\bigcup_i \front{r_i}$,\;\,
$\front k=\{k\}$ otherwise.
\emph{(A plain row is itself; an alternation splits into its branches.)}

\item \textbf{Sequence normalizer} $\seqn(r,k)$ --- ``the row $r$ followed by
$k$,'' with units erased and right-associated:\;
$\seqn(0,k)=0$,\;\,$\seqn(1,k)=k$,\;\,$\seqn(r_1 r_2,k)=\seqn(r_1,\seqn(r_2,k))$;\;
for an atom $a$ (letter / star / alternation):\;
$\seqn(a,0)=0$,\;$\seqn(a,1)=a$,\;else $\seqn(a,k)=a\,k$.

\item \textbf{Accumulator} $\acc(r,k)$ --- the rows that one derivative of $r$
leaves in front of continuation $k$:
\[
\acc(0,k)=\acc(1,k)=\emptyset,\quad
\acc(c,k)=\front k,\quad
\acc(\Sigma rs,k)=\bigcup_i\acc(r_i,k),
\]
\[
\acc(r_1 r_2,\,k)=\acc\bigl(r_1,\seqn(r_2,k)\bigr)\,\cup\,\acc(r_2,k),
\qquad
\acc(r^*,k)=\acc\bigl(r,\seqn(r^*,k)\bigr).
\]
\emph{(A letter hands its whole continuation-frontier forward; a sequence
accumulates the left part against the normalized $r_2k$ plus the right part
against $k$; a star re-enters itself. This recursion --- one letter importing a
whole frontier, balanced only across siblings --- is exactly what made the
D law hard.)}

\item \textbf{Weight} $\zw(r)$ --- letters, $+1$ per star; always
$\zw(r)\le\rs{r}$:\;
$\zw(0)=\zw(1)=0$,\;$\zw(c)=1$,\;
$\zw(r_1 r_2)=\zw(\Sigma rs)=\text{sum}$,\;
$\zw(r^*)=1+\zw(r)$,\;$\zw(r^{\{n\}})=n\,(1+\zw(r))$.

\item \textbf{Static row universe} $\Rows(r)=\{r\}\cup\front r\cup\bigcup_q\front q$,
the union over all Antimirov sub-derivative terms $q$ of $r$.
\emph{(Every row that can ever appear in front of $r$; a finite set.)}

\end{itemize}
